THE THROUGH-LINE

Different stack. Same problem.

How do you make a crowd of independent parts behave like one dependable system?

Every role moved one layer outward: radio state, device efficiency, then agent behavior. The through-line is orchestration - making independent parts coordinate under real constraints without making the user carry the complexity.

RRC and MCP live generations apart, but they rhyme. In 5G, RRC helps a device and network negotiate capabilities and establish a connection; in Office AI, MCP helps a model discover what an application can do and invoke it. I have had the unusual luck of working on both boundaries.

OPERATING NOTES

The habits the stack could not change.

Make concurrency visible.

If work can happen in parallel, the architecture should say so plainly. Hidden concurrency becomes surprise; explicit concurrency becomes leverage.

Trust the second run.

A perfect demo is a beginning, not evidence. I care about evaluation, observability and failure paths that keep telling the truth after novelty wears off.

Move complexity away from the user.

Whether the input is an ink stroke or a request for a deck, the person should meet their intention - not the machinery required to fulfill it.
